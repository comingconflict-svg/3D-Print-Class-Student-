## Safety
1. The nozzle and bed burn. Do not touch metal inside the chamber during or right after a print.
2. Keep the door closed while the toolhead is moving.
3. Hair, hoodies, and fingers stay outside the machine.
4. If a print looks wrong, say pause. Do not yank the print or pull filament out of a hot nozzle.
5. Scraper points down and away from your other hand.
6. Use only the filament the teacher loaded.
