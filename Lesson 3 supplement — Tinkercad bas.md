## What Tinkercad is
Tinkercad is free 3D design in a web browser. You drag simple shapes onto a flat workplane, size them in millimeters, and combine them.

## Quick map
- Workplane — the floor you build on
- Shapes — box, cylinder, roof, text
- Solid — real plastic
- Hole — a cookie cutter that subtracts when you Group
- Align — line shapes up
- Group — glue solids together, or cut holes
- Export — download STL for OrcaSlicer
